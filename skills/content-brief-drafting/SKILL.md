# content-brief-drafting

## Input
`business_name` and `website` identify the account; `topic` is optional.

## Output
A `draft` for owner review and `placeholders` for any unverified claims.

## Constraints
- No social posting, scheduling, outreach or external account modifications.
- Supplied account details do not authorize fact claims. Ask for evidence or
  leave a bracketed placeholder instead of fabricating product claims.
- The output is a draft until Laurel reviews and approves it.
